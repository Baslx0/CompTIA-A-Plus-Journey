## Key concepts

### Docking station vs. port replicator

A **docking station** expands a laptop into more of a desktop-style setup. It can provide displays, Ethernet, USB devices, power delivery, and other peripherals through one connection.

A **port replicator** is simpler and is mainly used to add or duplicate ports.

Older docks were often model-specific, while many modern USB-C or Thunderbolt docks work across multiple systems.

### USB-C and Lightning

- **USB-C** is a widely used reversible connector.
- **Lightning** is Apple's proprietary reversible connector.

### Bluetooth

Bluetooth provides short-range wireless connectivity between nearby devices and is commonly associated with a **PAN — Personal Area Network**.

### NFC

**Near Field Communication (NFC)** is a very short-range wireless technology. A common example is contactless payment.

### Hotspot vs. tethering

Both can share a phone's Internet connection.

- **Hotspot:** usually shares the connection over Wi-Fi.
- **Tethering:** commonly refers to a direct connection such as USB, although the term can be broader.

### Input accessories

- **Stylus:** pen-like input for writing or drawing.
- **Drawing pad:** surface used with a stylus for drawing/input.
- **Trackpad:** touch-sensitive pointing surface built into many laptops.
- **TrackPoint:** pointing stick located between keys on some laptop keyboards.
