# Resources

These are the main resources I use during my CompTIA A+ study.

## Official CompTIA A+ exam objectives

I use the official objectives as the roadmap for coverage.

- Core 1: **220-1201**
- Core 2: **220-1202**

Official site: https://www.comptia.org/certifications/a

The objectives tell me what CompTIA expects me to know. They are not a full course, so I use other resources to learn the concepts.

## Professor Messer

**Primary learning source**

https://www.professormesser.com/

I follow the objectives in sequence and use the videos as the main explanation source.

## ExamCompass

**Independent knowledge checks**

https://www.examcompass.com/

I use the quizzes after studying a topic to find gaps in my understanding.

I do not treat the quizzes as a question bank to memorize.

## ChatGPT

**Clarification and practice**

I use ChatGPT for:

- explaining concepts that did not fully click;
- comparing similar technologies;
- troubleshooting scenarios;
- PBQ-style practice;
- short active-recall checks;
- reviewing gaps found during testing.

## IT HomeLab

**Hands-on practice**

https://github.com/Baslx0/IT-HomeLab

My HomeLab repository contains practical work involving Windows, networking, virtualization, Active Directory, DNS, troubleshooting, and documentation.

I keep the HomeLab separate so this repository can stay focused on the certification learning journey.
